FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is VidGrab free?

The MVP interface is free to use. Monetization can be added later with compliant advertising and optional premium features.

Which platforms are supported?

The interface is designed for YouTube, YouTube Shorts, Instagram and Facebook URLs. Actual downloading requires a backend integration that follows each platform's rules.

Can I download any video?

No. Download only content you own or have permission to download, and follow the applicable platform terms and copyright law.
